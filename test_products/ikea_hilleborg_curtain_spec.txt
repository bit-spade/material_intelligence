PRODUCT TECHNICAL DATASHEET & ENVIRONMENTAL SPECIFICATION

Product Name: IKEA HILLEBORG
Product Type: Room-Darkening / Blackout Curtains
Category: Textile / Window Treatments
Manufacturer: IKEA of Sweden AB, SE-343 81 Älmhult

1. MATERIAL COMPOSITION & SOURCING
- Total Composition: 100% Polyester (100% Recycled)
- Raw Material Source: Certified post-consumer recycled PET bottles (approx. 118 PET 0.5L bottles repurposed per pair).
- Mono-material: Yes, 100% single-fiber polyester construction.
- Dyeing Method: Solution-dyed yarn, significantly reducing water consumption and dye bath chemical discharge compared to conventional piece-dyeing.

2. ENVIRONMENTAL COMPLIANCE & CHEMICAL SAFETY
- PFAS / PFC Content: 100% Free from fluorinated compounds (PFAS/PFC-free). IKEA strictly prohibits PFAS chemicals across all textile treatments.
- Flame Retardant Treatments: Free from chemical flame retardants. Complies with general household flammability standards via fiber structure without added organohalogen or organophosphate finishes.
- Certification: Manufactured in accordance with IKEA IWAY forestry & chemical standards and OEKO-TEX Standard 100 Class II (tested for harmful substances, pesticide residues, heavy metals, and formaldehyde).
- ISO Certification: Production partner facilities operate under certified ISO 14001 Environmental Management Systems.

3. CARE, MAINTENANCE & DURABILITY
- Cleaning: Machine washable at home (max 30°C, mild process).
- Chemical Dry Cleaning: Not required (Do not dry clean).
- Abrasion Resistance: Exceeds 25,000 Martindale cycles (suitable for domestic and hospitality curtain use).
- Light Fastness: Scale 5 (high resistance to color fading from sunlight exposure).
- Shrinkage: Max 4%.

4. CIRCULARITY & END-OF-LIFE
- Recyclability: 100% mono-material polyester construction enables mechanical and chemical textile-to-textile recycling.
- Take-back: Covered under IKEA circular hub / furniture buy-back and textile recycling collection points in selected markets.
- Packaging: Minimalist packaging made from 100% recycled paperboard.
